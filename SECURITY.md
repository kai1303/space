# Reporting a security issue

If you think someone could read another workspace’s messages, use a connector without its owner’s approval, or otherwise cross a security boundary, ask for a private way to reach a maintainer.

1. [Open a contact-only issue](https://github.com/kai1303/space/issues/new?title=Security%20contact%20request&body=I%20would%20like%20to%20report%20a%20security%20issue%20privately.%20Please%20provide%20a%20private%20contact%20method.). The prefilled title and body are enough. Everything in this issue is public.
2. Wait for a repository maintainer to provide a private contact method. Keep affected endpoints, logs, screenshots, and reproduction details out of the issue and its comments.
3. Through that private channel, share the Space release tag or commit, the affected component, what access was required, and what happened. Use sample accounts and redact credentials, pairing codes, signed URLs, and workspace content.

For ordinary bugs or setup problems, use the [issue chooser](https://github.com/kai1303/space/issues/new/choose).
