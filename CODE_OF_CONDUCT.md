# Space code of conduct

People of every background and experience level are welcome to ask questions, report problems, review changes, and contribute to Space. These expectations apply to everyone participating in the project’s issues, pull requests, and other project spaces.

Explain disagreements with examples. Give people room to ask for clarification and to correct mistakes. Harassment, threats, discriminatory remarks, unwanted sexual attention, and sharing someone’s private information without permission are grounds for moderation.

## Reporting and moderation

To flag a GitHub issue, pull request, or comment, open its menu and choose **Report content**. Select **Report to repository admins** when available. You can also report abuse directly to GitHub Support, including when the concern involves a maintainer. Include a link to the content and keep personal details out of public replies. [GitHub reporting options](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam)

Maintainers may ask someone to change their behavior, remove harmful content, or restrict participation based on the severity and repetition of the conduct.
